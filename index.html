Pennsylvania Election: Key Deadlines

General election, Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Last checked 2026-10-03.

Deadlines (Eastern Time)

MilestoneWhenTime leftWhat it means
Voter registration deadlineMonday, October 19, 2026
11:59 p.m. EDT		 Online applications must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. Paper applications must be received by the county office by the deadline. There is no registration at the polls on Election Day. Sources: [1] [2] [3]
Mail-in / absentee ballot application deadlineTuesday, October 27, 2026
5:00 p.m. EDT		 Your county election office must RECEIVE the application by 5:00 p.m. A postmark is not enough. You can also apply, complete, and return a ballot in one visit to your county office through this deadline. Sources: [1] [2] [3]
Completed mail ballot must be receivedTuesday, November 3, 2026
8:00 p.m. EST		 Your county must RECEIVE the ballot by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. A postmark by Election Day does not count. Sign and date the outer envelope. A mail ballot cannot be dropped off at a polling place. Sources: [1] [2] [3]
Polls open 7:00 a.m. / close 8:00 p.m.Tuesday, November 3, 2026
8:00 p.m. EST		 Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. If you were sent a mail ballot but want to vote in person, bring the ballot and envelopes to surrender at your polling place; otherwise you may vote a provisional ballot. Sources: [1] [2] [3]

Corroborated across multiple sources on the as-of date. Confirm against the Pennsylvania Department of State (vote.pa.gov) before acting; deadlines can be changed by law or court order.

Who can vote

  • At least 18 years old on or before Election Day
  • A U.S. citizen for at least 30 days before the election
  • A Pennsylvania resident, living in your election district for at least 30 days before the election
