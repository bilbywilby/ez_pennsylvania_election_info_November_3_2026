## **3. FIRST-TIME VOTER ESSENTIALS**

### **Required ID at Polling Place**

| Voter Type | ID Required | Acceptable Forms |
|------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Returning Voter (same precinct)** | **NOT required** | Bring sample ballot or memory of precinct |
| **First-Time Voter (at precinct)** | **REQUIRED** | Photo ID or Non-Photo ID (see below) |
| **Moved Recently** | Recommended | Proof of address (utility bill, lease, etc.) |
| **Name Changed** | Recommended | Marriage certificate, court order, new ID |

### **Acceptable PHOTO ID:**
- PA Driver's License / PennDOT ID
- U.S. Passport
- U.S. Military ID
- Student ID (from accredited PA school)
- Employee ID (from government or private entity)

### **Acceptable NON-PHOTO ID:**
- Voter Registration Card
- Utility bill (current month)
- Bank statement
- Paycheck stub
- Government check
- Non-photo driver's license

## **4. CURBSIDE & SPECIAL VOTING OPTIONS**

### **Curbside Voting Details**

| Feature | Information |
|---------|-------------|
| **Eligibility** | Any voter who cannot physically enter polling place |
| **How It Works** | Call polling place from car; two poll workers bring ballot |
| **Hours** | Same as polling hours (7 AM – 8 PM) |
| **ID Requirements** | Same as in-person voting |
| **Privacy** | Vote in your vehicle without entering building |

### **Designated Agent for Mail-in Ballot Return:**

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Who Can Be Agent** | Registered PA voter (can be family member) |
| **Cannot Be Agent** | Employer, union representative, candidate (except immediate family) |
| **Maximum Ballots** | Limited by PA law (typically 1–2 additional ballots) |
| **Documentation** | Agent must show photo ID and may need to sign affidavit |

## **5. PROVISIONAL VOTING INFORMATION**

### **When Provisional Ballots Are Used**

| Situation | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Your name is not on voter registration list | Registration error or recent move |
| Eligibility challenged at polling place | Someone questions your eligibility |
| Didn't bring required ID (first-time voters) | Missing documentation |
| Wrong precinct but right county | Need to cast partial ballot |
| Voter ID issues unresolved | Cannot verify identity at polls |

### **Provisional Ballot Process:**

| Step | Timeline | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **1. Cast Provisional Ballot** | Election Day | Complete provisional affidavit at polling place |
| **2. Ballot Sealed** | Election Day | Placed in secure envelope; not counted immediately |
| **3. Verification** | Within 3–7 days | Board of Elections verifies eligibility |
| **4. Result Notification** | Up to 15 days | Check status at `pavoterservices.pa.gov` |

### **Tracking Your Provisional Ballot:**

| Method | URL / Contact |
|--------|--------------|
| **Online Tracking** | `pavoterservices.pa.gov` → Check Ballot Status |
| **Phone Inquiry** | Lehigh County Board of Elections: (610) 782-3194 |
| **Results Timeline** | Usually available within 10 days of Election Day |

## **H. REFERENDUM QUESTION BREAKDOWN**

### **Question 1: Election Commission Expansion**

| Dimension | YES Position | NO Position |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Representation** | Unaffiliated voters gain guaranteed seat | Maintains two-party structure |
| **Conflict Prevention** | Removes County Executive from election board when running for office | Current system creates inherent conflict |
| **Partisan Balance** | 2-2-1 structure prevents party bloc voting | Traditional 1-1-Executive model has worked |
| **Administrative Cost** | Adds 2 stipends + overhead (~$15–25K/year estimated) | No additional positions = lower cost |
| **Selection Process** | Opens public applications beyond party nominations | Party-controlled appointments ensure accountability |
| **Unique in PA** | First guaranteed independent seat on election board | Standard Pennsylvania model |

### **Question 2: Legal Newspaper Notices**

| Dimension | YES Position | NO Position |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Cost Savings** | Reduces duplicate advertising expenses (estimate: $50–150K/year savings) | N/A |
| **Modern Media** | Aligns with shift to digital consumption | Assumes print still necessary for transparency |
| **Transparency** | Still requires publication in 1 general-circulation newspaper | Argues dual newspapers maximize visibility |
| **Local Press Impact** | Potential revenue loss for community papers | Legal notices support struggling local journalism |
| **Demographic Access** | Acknowledges digital accessibility | Protects elderly/print-dependent communities |

### **Question 3: Retirement Board Representation**

| Dimension | YES Position | NO Position |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Expertise Matching** | Assign member with finance/investment/HR background | Board Chair has ultimate accountability regardless |
| **Workload Distribution** | Prevents bottlenecks for Board Chair | Maintains clear chain of responsibility |
