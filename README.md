**Critical Context for Unaffiliated Voters:**

1. **Super PAC Limitations**: Congressional Leadership Fund and House Majority PAC operate with less transparent donor disclosure than candidate committees — you may not know all the ultimate funders

2. **Coordinated vs. Independent**: NRCC/DCCC can coordinate spending with candidates; Super PACs technically cannot but often share strategies

3. **Downballot Effect**: Shapiro's $5.6M injection into Democratic coffers for downballot races (per Spotlight PA) suggests he's using gubernatorial victory to influence state legislature outcomes

4. **Regional Influence**: Jeff Yass (Pennsylvania billionaire) is the Republican counterweight to Shapiro's national Democratic donor base

## 3. DECISION MATRIX FOR REFERENDUM QUESTIONS

### **Question 1: Election Commission Expansion**

| Factor | Argument for YES | Argument for NO |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Representation** | Unaffiliated voters (~growing segment in Lehigh County) gain designated voice on board | Existing 3-member structure has functioned adequately |
| **Partisan Balance** | 2-2-1 structure prevents either major party from controlling election certification alone | Concern about how "Independent" member gets vetted/appointed |
| **Conflict of Interest** | Removes County Executive from board when that person may be on ballot as candidate | N/A (current system has County Executive on board regardless) |
| **Administrative Cost** | Additional stipends and positions increase county overhead | Added two board positions = increased administrative costs |
| **Workload Distribution** | More members help handle mail-in ballots, polling place settings, registration appeals | Status quo sufficient; no demonstrated need for expansion |

**What to weigh if you're unaffiliated:** Do you value having guaranteed representation on the election board more than accepting increased administrative costs?

---

### **Question 2: Legal Newspaper Notices**

| Factor | Argument for YES | Argument for NO |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Taxpayer Costs** | Reduces duplicate advertising expenses; estimated savings not quantified in available documents | — |
| **Transparency** | Acknowledges shift to digital communication; county web portals supplement notices | Reducing from 2 to 1 newspaper decreases visibility of government actions |
| **Accessibility** | — | Elderly residents and communities relying on local print media lose coverage |
| **Local Media Impact** | — | Legal notices provide revenue stream for community newspapers |

**What to weigh if you're unaffiliated:** Do you prioritize cost savings and modern media consumption habits, or maintaining broader public notice reach through multiple publications?

---

### **Question 3: Retirement Board Representation**

| Factor | Argument for YES | Argument for NO |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Expertise Matching** | Can assign Commissioner with financial/investment expertise to pension oversight | Board Chair (top legislative leader) should remain directly accountable for pensions |
| **Administrative Efficiency** | Prevents bottlenecks; Board Chair can focus on broader legislative duties | Unnecessary charter change; existing system works adequately |
| **Flexibility** | Commissioners can delegate to whoever has most relevant time/skills | Concern about reducing accountability at highest leadership level |

**What to weigh if you're unaffiliated:** Does specialized expertise matter more than direct accountability from the Board Chair for pension fund decisions?
