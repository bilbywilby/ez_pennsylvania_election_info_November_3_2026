## 3. DECISION MATRIX FOR REFERENDUM QUESTIONS

### **Question 1: Election Commission Expansion**

| Factor | Argument for YES | Argument for NO |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Representation** | Unaffiliated voters (~growing segment in Lehigh County) gain designated voice on board | Existing 3-member structure has functioned adequately |
| **Partisan Balance** | 2-2-1 structure prevents either major party from controlling election certification alone | Concern about how "Independent" member gets vetted/appointed |
| **Conflict of Interest** | Removes County Executive from board when that person may be on ballot as candidate | N/A (current system has County Executive on board regardless) |
| **Administrative Cost** | Additional stipends and positions increase county overhead | Added two board positions = increased administrative costs |
| **Workload Distribution** | More members help handle mail-in ballots, polling place settings, registration appeals | Status quo sufficient; no demonstrated need for expansion |

**What to weigh if you're unaffiliated:** Do you value having guaranteed representation on the election board more than accepting increased administrative costs?
