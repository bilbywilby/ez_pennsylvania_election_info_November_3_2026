## **HOW TO FIND YOUR EXACT POLLING PLACE**

### **Step-by-Step Instructions:**

| Step | Action | What You Need |
|------|--------|---------------|
| **1** | Go to PA Voter Services Portal | Computer or smartphone with internet |
| **2** | Click "Polling Place Information" | — |
| **3** | Select **Lehigh County** from dropdown menu | Your county of residence |
| **4** | Enter your **municipality** (e.g., Allentown) | Your city/town name |
| **5** | Enter **street address**, house number, and zip code | Your full residential address |
| **6** | Click "Search" or "Submit" | — |
| **7** | View results: polling location address, district, ward/precinct | Your complete voting info |

### **URL Shortcut:**
`pavoterservices.pa.gov/pages/pollingplaceinfo.aspx`

### **Alternative Methods:**

| Method | How to Access | Best For |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Check Voter ID Card** | Look at your physical voter registration card | Quick reference |
| **Call County Office** | (610) 782-3194 | Phone assistance |
| **Ask at Polls** | On Election Day, if wrong location, vote provisional | Last-minute discovery |

### **What You'll Receive:**

| Information | Example Format |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Polling Place Name** | "Allentown High School Gymnasium" |
| **Full Address** | "1501 Hamilton St, Allentown, PA 18104" |
| **Ward/Precinct Number** | "Ward 12-04" |
| **State Senate District** | "District 14" |
| **State House District** | "District 22" |
| **Judge Retention** | Any retention judges for your area |
| **Directions/Map** | Often includes Google Maps link |

### **Pro Tips:**

| Tip | Why It Matters |
|-----|----------------|
| **Verify 1–2 weeks before Election Day** | Polling locations can change |
| **Screenshot or print results** | Don't rely on phone memory |
| **Note parking instructions** | Some locations have limited parking |
| **Check curbside availability** | If you need ADA accommodations |
